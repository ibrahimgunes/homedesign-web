Privacy Policy

App: Home & Interior Design AI · Developer: FLZ Software Solutions · Effective date: November 1, 2026

Home & Interior Design AI ("the app") is developed and operated by FLZ Software Solutions ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, where it is processed, how long it is kept and how you can delete it.

In short: the photo of your room, house or garden is used only to create the design you ask for, and the uploaded copy is deleted from our servers as soon as the design is ready. We do not sell data, show ads, or use your photos to train AI models. You can delete any result, or all of your data, inside the app at any time.

1. What we collect

  • Your photo. When you create a design, the photo you choose is uploaded so the design can be generated. For brush tools (wall, replace, remove) a copy with your brush marks, or a black-and-white mask of them, is uploaded too.
  • Your choices sent with each request: room type, style, wall colour, floor, object, and any text you type.
  • Generated images. Your designs are stored so you can see them in History.
  • An anonymous account. The app signs you in anonymously. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • A device identifier stored on your device, used only to give the free welcome credits once per device.
  • A notification token, if you allow notifications, used only to tell you when a design is ready.
  • Purchase information. When you subscribe, Apple sends us a signed record of the transaction (product, price, currency, transaction ID, expiry) so we can add your credits. We never see your payment details.
  • App activity within our own app (for example: a design was started, the Pro page was viewed), stored with your anonymous account to improve the app. We do not use third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • Messages you send us through "Contact support", "Report" or the feedback card.

2. Processing on your device

Your original photo is kept only on your iPhone, so History can show before and after. If you type a request in a language other than English, it may be translated on your device by Apple's Translation framework before it is sent.

3. How photos are processed

Uploaded photos are stored temporarily in Google Firebase (Google Cloud) and sent to our own image-generation servers (GPU servers rented from RunPod) running an open-source AI model. They are not sent to third-party AI services and are not used to train any model.

4. Retention and deletion

  • The uploaded photo (and its brush copy or mask) is deleted from our servers as soon as the design is finished, or immediately if it fails. If a generation fails, your credit is refunded.
  • Generated designs are kept in your private storage until you delete them.
  • Delete a single design by pressing and holding it in History. This removes it from our servers and its original from your device.
  • Settings → Delete my data removes all of your designs and history from our servers immediately.
  • Images you saved to your Photos library or shared are on your device or with the services you chose, and are not affected.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your data. It is processed only by our infrastructure providers — Google Firebase (storage, database, functions, notifications), RunPod (image generation) and Apple (purchases, translation) — to run the app, or disclosed if required by law.

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Only your own account can read your designs and history. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data.

7. Children

The app is not intended for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will delete the data.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or KVKK), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, or to object to processing. You can delete your data directly in the app; for any other request, write to us from Settings → Contact support.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version; important changes will be announced in the app.
