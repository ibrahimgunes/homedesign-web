How do I get the best result?

Shoot in daylight, from a corner, with the whole room in the frame. For brush tools, paint generously over the whole object.

A design failed. Did I lose a credit?

No. If a design fails, the credit is returned automatically.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone → your name → Subscriptions → Home Design Pro → Cancel. You keep Pro until the end of the current period.

How do I restore a purchase?

In the app: Settings → Restore purchases, or "Restore" on the Pro page.

How do I delete my data?

In the app: Settings → Delete my data. See the Privacy Policy.
